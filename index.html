Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Vriksha handles information when you use the app.

Data Collection

Vriksha does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

All focus session data, tree growth progress, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics

Vriksha may use privacy-friendly, anonymized analytics strictly to understand app stability and performance, such as crash reports or feature usage.

This data cannot identify you personally and is never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Permissions

Vriksha requests only essential permissions, such as notifications (for focus reminders) and vibration (for haptic feedback). These permissions are used solely for core app functionality and can be managed in your device settings.

In-App Purchases

Vriksha offers optional one-time in-app purchases to unlock additional trees. All payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children’s Privacy

Vriksha is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
